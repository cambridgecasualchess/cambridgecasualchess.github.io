Christmas Tournament 2025

Another fantastic Christmas tournament, this time at our regular venue, the Grain & Hop store.

Although the turnout (14 people) was smaller than last year, the competitian was by no means less fierce.

With 6 rounds of 10 minute games, friendly banter and puzzles between games it was an excellent day of chess.

Thanks to everyone who attended!
